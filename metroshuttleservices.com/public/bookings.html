Common Questions

Booking a shuttle with Metro.

How do I book shuttle service to the Miami cruise port?

Use the booking portal above with your pickup address, ship name or terminal, and departure time, or call Customer Service and we'll confirm a pickup window for PortMiami or Port Everglades.

Do you offer shuttle service from FLL to the Miami cruise port?

Yes. Metro runs private shuttle service from Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport directly to PortMiami and Port Everglades, with vehicles sized for solo travelers up to full groups.

Can I book a private shuttle service in Miami for a group?

Yes. Passenger vans and Sprinters are available for larger parties, families, and events. Include your passenger count when booking so we assign the right vehicle.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as soon as your travel or cruise dates are confirmed. For cruise port pickups and drop-offs, earlier booking helps guarantee availability during peak sailing days.
