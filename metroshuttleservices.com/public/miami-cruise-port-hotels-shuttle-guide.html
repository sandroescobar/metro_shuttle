Planning A Pre-Cruise Stay

Many hotel shuttles run on limited schedules.

A lot of hotels near the Miami cruise port offer a shuttle to PortMiami, but schedules are often limited to a few set departure times, first-come availability, or advance sign-up. If your flight lands late, your group is large, or your sailing time doesn't match the hotel's shuttle window, a private option keeps you on your own schedule.

Downtown Miami Hotels

Closest to PortMiami, often the shortest ride — but shuttle windows can be tight on busy sailing mornings.

Miami Airport Area Hotels

Convenient for late flight arrivals the night before a cruise, with a longer ride to the terminal.

Fort Lauderdale Hotels

Common for cruisers flying into FLL — private shuttle covers the longer distance to PortMiami directly.
