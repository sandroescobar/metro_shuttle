Common route pricing

Rates are per vehicle, not per passenger, and apply in either direction. Prices below are estimates to help you plan.

Route SUV 10-passenger van 14-passenger van
Miami Airport (MIA) ↔ PortMiami $90 $130 $170
Miami Airport (MIA) ↔ hotels near the cruise port $90 $130 $170
Miami Airport (MIA) ↔ South Beach $100 $150 $180
Fort Lauderdale Airport (FLL) ↔ PortMiami $130 $180 $250

These are estimates, not final quotes. Your confirmed price depends on passenger count, luggage, pickup time, and any additional stops. Routes not listed here, including Port Everglades, group charters, and hourly bookings, are quoted per trip. Call or request a quote and we will confirm the exact price before you book.

What is included

Every Metro booking is private and point to point.

The whole vehicle

Rates are per vehicle. You are not sharing the ride with other passengers, and the price does not change if your group is smaller than the vehicle's capacity.

Door to door

Direct pickup and drop-off at your terminal, hotel, or address, with no additional stops on the way.

Luggage space

Vehicles are matched to your bag count, not just your passenger count. Tell us what you are travelling with when you book.
